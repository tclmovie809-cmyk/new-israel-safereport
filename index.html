🆘 Need Immediate Help?

🚨 If you are in immediate danger

Do not wait for an online report to be reviewed. Move to a safe place and contact a trusted adult, teacher, school authority, parent/guardian, or appropriate emergency service.

People you can approach:

  • 👩‍🏫 Trusted Teacher
  • 🧑‍🏫 School Counselor / Guidance Personnel
  • 🏫 School Administrator
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent or Guardian
  • 🤝 Trusted Adult

🔒 Privacy & Safety

Protect your information

  • Never share your password.
  • Do not post private reports publicly.
  • Only provide information needed for the report.
  • Do not forward private photos or messages unnecessarily.
  • Use a trusted adult or school authority when you need help.
